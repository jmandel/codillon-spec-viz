04 · Fact ribbons on connections — provenance and decay of knowledge · a refinement-typed Codillon prototype

Codillon already draws every value’s journey as a connection from the producer’s output shape to the consumer’s input cup. Here each connection becomes a ribbon that also carries the facts the checker knows about that value: one thin stripe per fact (parity = comb, bound = band, equality = knot). As a value passes through an instruction a fact is born (✦; i32.shl 1 births “even”), carried (even + even), or dies — drawn as the stripe fraying out at that instruction. A cup that requires a fact wears a small badge in the right margin: filled when the stripe arrives, hollow when it does not. Facts that do not ride on a stack value (the arm of an if, a loop invariant, a local carried across lines) flow down fact rails at the far right and tap into the badges or values that use them. To find a bug, follow the darkred dash-dot ghost lane up from a hollow badge to the darkred fray: that instruction is where the fact was lost. On the slider, a stripe or badge whose fact is actually false at the current step turns solid darkred, from the producer onward.

A — The primitive in isolation

A.1 Born · carried · dies (parity)

The comb stripe is born at i32.shl (✦). With i32.const 2 it rides through the add into $half’s cup, whose badge is filled. Switch to i32.const 1: the stripe frays at the add (and an offset “odd” comb is born), the badge goes hollow, and since every x is a witness the badge is solid darkred. With local.get $y the badge is refuted but y = 2 does not exhibit it: dashed outline until a witness is replayed.

A.2 A bound is a band

The mask births a band; a looser bound is a thicker band. $slot’s cup needs “<8”: with mask 15 that stripe was never born (ghost lane up to the i32.and), and k = 13 turns the badge solid darkred at run time.

A.3 A knot, and an assumption that turns out false at run time

$half is exported, so its precondition is checked dynamically at the host boundary (slategray dotted badge on line 2). Inside, “even” is born from the precondition and the shift births the knot v+v=x. Drag x to an odd value: the precondition fails, and every stripe that rested on it turns darkred from the producer onward. The other two settings show an unknown fact (indigo, needs a nonlinear lemma; slategray “held on this run” once executed) and an assumed one (ghosted).
