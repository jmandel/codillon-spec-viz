# Review of Refined Codillon (`artifacts/00-refined-codillon.html`)

**Overall verdict.** `00-refined-codillon.html` is a cohesive, high-craft piece of information design that succeeds at its hardest goal: extending Winstein’s no-error-panel, continuous-execution WebAssembly editor to specification checking without turning the margin into a theorem-prover console. The proven state is genuinely calm, the core visual primitives—the piecewise linear-log `i32` number-line shape, the darkred spill over an input cup’s rim, the inline synthetic proof hole (`𝄽`), and the on-demand integer ring and loop lap-chart lenses—earn their keep, and the unified interpreter reproduces all 144 fixture key frames on load. Nonetheless, several concrete defects must be fixed before it can stand as the definitive design specification: a factually inverted prose explanation and broken replay jump in Lesson 3’s Seed (a), a rendering bug where blamed fix-lines (`fixAlso`, such as line 17 `i32.gt_s` in Lesson 2) draw their fraction rule as *proven* and their cup spill as *unreplayed* even after replaying the witness, an overly narrow boundary check in the Memory Mirror lens that falsely labels `R` as `"open: mid-operation"` throughout read-only `$lookup` calls, micro-collisions around multi-operand refined shapes, and pedagogical leaps where a Wasm programmer is left without the bridge to Hoare logic or refinement types.

---

## A. Tutorial clarity

A reader who knows WebAssembly (stack effects, two’s-complement `i32`, `gt_s`/`gt_u`, linear memory, traps) will grasp the core premise—types as rectangles, refinements as shaded sub-regions, typechecking as spatial containment—within §§0–2. Where the prose falters is at the seams between Wasm execution and verification concepts.

1. **Unexplained shifts in refinement binder syntax (`$x` vs. `v` vs. `$h` vs. `v₇`).**
   §0 introduces `(func $half (param $x (i32 | $x % 2 == 0)) (result i32))` (*"the shape of `$x` on line 2 grows from 30 to 84 px [72 px in `rv00-top.png`]"*). Two sentences later, §1 switches without explanation to bare `v` and `n` without WAT `$` sigils: *"A refinement `(i32 | P)` carves out the region where `P` holds"*, with buttons `(i32 | 0 <= v)` and `(i32 | 0 <= v < n)`. §2 uses `$i` in `(param $i (i32 | 0 <= $i < 8))`, `v` in its chips (`1 <= v < 9`), and subscripted stack names (`v₇ < 8`, `v₁₅` in Lesson 2) inside holes. §3 introduces named result binders absent from WAT (`(result $h (i32 | $h + $h == $x))`), while §5 reverts to unnamed results and bare `v` even in parameters (`(param $i (i32 | 0 <= v && v < CAP))`). Deferring this to §7 *Limits* (*"Mixed syntax..."*) leaves the reader guessing; §1 needs one sentence stating that `v` (or the parameter/result name) binds the refined value, and `v₇`/`v₁₅` name the stack value produced on line 7 or 15.

2. **Contradictory setup in §1 ("Six states, one cup") and undefined "vacuous".**
   §1 states: *"Below, the same cup requires `0 ≤ v < 8` and is fed `1 ≤ v ≤ 8`, so the value 8 spills over the rim. The six panels are six things the checker can say about that spill."* Because `[1, 8] ⊆ [0, 8)` is false, the checker could never report **proven** about that spill; `sixPanel()` secretly changes the provided set to `[1, 7]` in panel 1 (`rv00-s1.png`), contradicting the prose. Panel 6 introduces **`assumed / vacuous`** (*"ghosted to 50%, hollow, `○`"*), without defining what a *vacuous* obligation is (a check under contradictory assumptions or an unreachable path) or why it shares a state with `(@assume P)`.

3. **§2 ("Reading a line") annotates the margin fraction, but leaves the proof hole and causal trails unexplained.**
   §2 says: *"In Refined Codillon the fraction is a sequent. Facts known about the inputs sit above the rule, facts claimed about the outputs sit below it, and the rule itself is the verdict. ... the cup on line 8 spills, and the editor inserts a hole after it that holds the counterexample."* While the 2× diagram in `rv00-s2.png` labels six parts of the margin fraction, it gives **zero callouts** to the much denser **proof hole** below line 8 (its hypothesis row `$i` and `v₇`, broken rule, conjunct boxes `0 <= v₇` vs. underlined darkred `v₇ < 8`, and suggestion chips). Worse, the vertical dashed lines with dots in the far-left gutter of `rv00-s2.png` (connecting lines 5 and 7 to the hole) are **never mentioned in §§0–6**, appearing only in §7 *Limits* (*"the causal trail: dashed lanes from a hole back to the lines its hypotheses came from"*).

4. **§3 (Lesson 1 · parity) glosses over occurrence typing and mathematical vs. Wasm modulo.**
   §3 says: *"Line 15 negates `is_even(x)`, and the `if` on line 16 sends odd numbers to the then-arm. In the else-arm `$x` is known to be even, so the comb on line 20 fits the cup on `call $half` (line 21) tooth for tooth."* Because `$x` has unrefined type `(param $x i32)` on line 12, `local.get $x` on line 13 has a plain 30×11 rectangle while `local.get $x` on line 20 has an 84×14 comb rectangle (`cur-L1-clean.png`); contrasting lines 13 and 20 is the clearest way to teach path-sensitive refinement. Moreover, Wasm’s `i32.rem_s` gives `-3 % 2 == -1`, whereas line 12’s spec writes `$x % 2 == 1` even for negative odd inputs (`half_or_neg1(-3)`). The prose never states the rule from `03-sample-programs.md` §1: spec expressions use mathematical integers ($\mathbb{Z}$) and Euclidean modulo (`%`), not 32-bit two’s-complement arithmetic.

5. **§4 (Lesson 2 · `isqrt`) under-explains loop-head counterexamples (CTIs) and hides the lemma statement.**
   Step 1 says: *"At the top of the loop the checker knows nothing about `$r`. It finds a loop-head state, n = 3 with r = 5, that leaves through the exit with a wrong answer."* A Wasm programmer will ask: *"Line 3 sets `$r` to 0, and for `n = 3` the loop exits at `r = 1`; how can `$r` be 5?"* Ten words is too brief to explain that verification cuts loops at `loop $grow` and treats modified locals as arbitrary unless constrained by `@invariant`. Step 2 says *"With r = 46340 the product on line 15 is 46341² = 2147488281"* without noting why `46340` appears ($\lfloor\sqrt{2^{31}-1}\rfloor = 46340$). Step 3 says *"The wrong instance shakes; `(@use sq_lt_sq $r 46341)` closes the hole"*, hiding `sq_lt_sq`’s signature (`0 <= x && 0 <= b && x*x < b*b ==> x < b`) inside a hover tooltip.

6. **§5 (Lesson 3) and §6 ("What the checker hands the editor") assume verification background.**
   In §5, the prose discusses strengthening clause `(iii)` of `R` while lines 7–11 (`R0`–`R4`) are folded by default (`rv00-L3.png`), so the reader never sees clause `(iii)` unless they click line 12 or hover `R(iii)`. In §6, the table maps `(i32 | P)` to *"a subtype `{v : Int32 // P v}`; the stack is a Σ-telescope of them"* without glossing "Σ-telescope" ("each slot’s predicate can refer to earlier slots").

## B. Visual design

### What succeeds
- **Calmness of the proven state (`cur-L1-clean.png`, `cur-L2-proven.png`).** Banning green, keeping lenses closed by default, hiding proven fact chips until hovered, and using a gray line number with a 1px rule gives a verified listing nearly the same visual weight as unrefined Codillon (`cur-s0.png`).
- **Piecewise number-line geometry and spill glyph (`rv00-s1.png`, `rv00-L1.png`).** Giving `[-8, 8]` the middle 60% linearly and `INT_MIN`/`INT_MAX` the outer 20% logarithmically makes both small indices (`0`, `1`, `8`) and overflow thresholds (`2¹⁶`, `2³¹−1`) readable inside one shape. Drawing `provided \ required` as a darkred hatched spill rising 3px above the cup rim—paired with a dotted darkred tail on the incoming stack curve—extends Winstein’s "frustrated connection" idiom naturally.
- **The integer ring and loop lap-chart lenses (`rv00-L2.png`, `cur-L2-proven.png`, `cur-L3-dweak.png`).** In `rv00-L2.png`, placing `0` at the bottom of the ring, the signed seam (`INT_MAX | INT_MIN`) at the top, `i32.gt_s` as a red cut at the top seam, and `46341² = 2147488281` spiraling past the top seam to `-2147479015` makes two’s-complement comparison bugs transparent. In the lap chart, separating cell *fill* (run: ivory vs. darkred) from cell *border* (checker), alongside the `⋯ 46 340 laps not run` hatch, cleanly distinguishes "passed on this run" from "proved."

### Where clutter, ambiguity, or visual bugs appear
1. **Blamed fix-lines (`fixAlso`) fail to update their cup spill, fraction rule, and fact chips (`rv00-L2-crop.png`).**
   On line 17 (`i32.gt_s`) in `rv00-L2-crop.png`, after replaying the S1 witness, the gutter shows a **solid** darkred `⚖` (from `S.fix['L17']`), yet the spill on line 17’s first input cup renders as a **dashed hollow outline** (`ghost` instead of solid darkred hatch), line 17’s fraction rule renders as an **intact gray proven line**, and its refuted fact chip (`= s`) stays **hidden** unless hovered. Because `H-back` sets `at: 'L23'` and `fixAlso: 'L17'`, `this.S.st['L17']` is undefined, so `fracSVG()` defaults `rst` to `'proven'` and `factsHTML()` suppresses the chip.

2. **Micro-collisions inside and between margin shapes (`rv00-L2-crop.png`, `cur-L2-nolemma.png`).**
   On line 17 (`i32.gt_s`), cup 0 and cup 1 (`2³¹−1`) sit 8px apart while cup 0’s overflow spill nub extends `7.2 px` past its right wall, colliding with cup 1’s left border in `rv00-L2-crop.png`. On lines 15–17, the stack curve from line 15’s output (`i32.mul`) to line 17’s cup 0 slices directly through line 16’s output shape (`local.get $n`). On line 15, the wrapped value dot sits on the left wall (`-2147479015`), while `-2.15e9` sits inside the shaded `[1, INT_MAX]` region on the right half.

3. **Epistemic state discrepancies between the legend and the listings (`rv00-s1.png`, `cur-L2-nolemma.png`, `cur-s2-gray.png`).**
   State 4 ("held on this run only", `dynamic`) never appears as a standalone state in any lesson—in Lesson 2 Tab 3 (`cur-L2-nolemma.png`), `∘ 4` appears only inside an `unknown` (indigo) hole. Meanwhile, both §1 (`rv00-s1.png`) and §7 (`rv00-s7.png`) show `𝄽?` in the gutter for `unknown`, yet `View.fill()` ignores `unknown` lines, leaving line 15 (`i32.mul`) in `cur-L2-nolemma.png` with a gray `15`. Under `gray=1` (`cur-s2-gray.png`), the chip borders for `refuted, not replayed` (`1.2px dashed darkred`) and `unknown` (`1.2px dashed indigo`) have identical dash patterns and nearly identical luminance.

4. **Left-gutter causal trails and weak lens affordances (`rv00-L2-crop.png`, `rv00-L3.png`, `cur-L2-S2-rel.png`).**
   Up to four dashed vertical lanes spaced 4px apart (`x = 6..18 px`) run down the far-left margin from hypothesis lines into each hole. In upstream Codillon (`00-codillon-today.md` §5), left-side vertical lines represent `block`/`loop`/`if` frames; repurposing the left margin for 30-row hypothesis trails (`rv00-L3.png` lines 12–41) makes dataflow look like block framing. Clickable function headers append a tiny `⋰` (`($root+1))) ⋰` on line 1 of `cur-L2-proven.png`) that looks like stray dust, while clickable `loop` lines have no affordance at all. In the Relation Plot lens (`cur-L2-S2-rel.png`), `↑ result` touches the top of the hatched `n<0` bar.

## C. Correctness

Checking the prose and visual states against `03-sample-programs.md` and `fixture.json` reveals four concrete correctness errors:

1. **False claim in §5 and `H-a` about Seed (a)’s execution order (`💣` on line 142 vs. `⚖` on line 151), plus a broken ternary in `View.replay()`.**
   - **Prose (§5):** *"Seed (a) drops the mask on the probe index: when you replay it, the run hits 💣 at line 142 before it ever reaches the ⚖ on 151. A bomb outranks a scale."* (`H-a` repeats: *"a run reaches the load at 142 with address 65536 first: 💣 outranks ⚖ there"*).
   - **Ground truth (`03-sample-programs.md` §B.7, bug `(a)`):** During `insert(15, 150)`, `h(15) = 7`. On probe `j = 0` (`i = 7`), line 140 `call $slot(7)` returns `65524` and line 142 `i32.load` (step 139) loads `st(7) = 1` (FULL) **without trapping**. Control enters `if` (line 143), computes unmasked `i = 8` on line 146, sets `j = 1` on line 150, and **executes `br $free` on line 151 at step 143**, where the loop invariant `i == at(k, j)` (`8 == 0`) **fails dynamically (`⚖`) first**! On the next iteration (`j = 1, i = 8`), line 140 `call $slot(8)` fails `$slot`’s precondition `0 <= i < CAP` at **step 145 (`⚖`)**, and only at **step 153** does line 142 `i32.load` trap with 💣 at address `65536` (see the two red ticks before step 153 on the slider in `cur-L3-a-tall.png`).
   - **Broken replay jump (`00-s5-arep.png`) and missing spill:** In `View.replay()` (line 1692), `if (run.halted === 'trap') i = run.recs.length - 1 >= 0 && h.key !== 'H-a' ? i : i;` has identical branches (`? i : i`), so clicking `▸ replay` on Seed (a) stops at step 143 (line 151) rather than step 153, and **no 💣 glyph is drawn anywhere** (`00-s5-arep.png`). Even at step 153 (`cur-L3-a-tall.png`), 💣 is on line 142 while `⚖` is on lines 147 and 151, so they never compete on the same line. Moreover, `03-sample-programs.md` §B.7 identifies Seed (a)’s static failure as **line 140 (`pre of $slot: 0 <= i < CAP`)**, but `PROGS.hash.derive()` hardcodes `ann.L139` to `[0, 8)` even when `f.code === 'a'`, hiding line 140’s spill.

2. **The Memory Mirror lens falsely reports `"R open: mid-operation, R may be false"` at step 241 of Seed (b) (`rv00-L3.png`).**
   §5 states: *"Seed (b) makes `$find` stop at a TOMB as if it were EMPTY. `R` stays true the whole time."* Yet in `rv00-L3.png` (at step 241, `lookup:72 call $find (return)`, where `▸ replay` lands for Seed (b)), the Mirror lens says **`R open: mid-operation, R may be false and is not checked here`** and grays out `R(o)`–`R(iv)`. `mirrorLens()` checks `(run.boundaries || []).includes(r.s)` where `run.boundaries` only records top-level call returns (`61, 170, 218, 247`), ignoring `$find`’s return and ignoring that `$lookup` and `$find` are read-only. Moreover, because `$lookup` (lines 67–83) is folded in `rv00-L3.png`, the navy execution arrow `→` at step 241 (`lookup:72`) is hidden inside the folded block.

3. **Lesson 2’s second suggestion chip (`SPEC: tighten requires to $n < 2147395600`) leaves stale overflow and spill marks on lines 15 and 17.**
   Clicking the `SPEC` chip in Tab 2 (`signed`) and accepting `(@use sq_lt_sq $r 46340)` closes all holes, but `isqrt.derive()` keeps `cap = 46340` on line 15 (drawing an overflow nub past `INT_MAX`) and keeps line 17’s cup spill and `'refuted'` chip, even though `$n < 2147395600 = 46340^2` bounds `$r <= 46339` and $(r+1)^2 \le 46340^2 \le 2^{31}-1$.

4. **Lesson 3’s prose for Seed (d) conflates state `d` with state `dweak` (`cur-L3-d.png` vs. `cur-L3-dweak.png`).**
   §5 states: *"Seed (d) lets the eighth insert through. The ring closes, `$find` probes forever, the lap chart’s measure staircase drops below zero, and the hole holds the corrected clause (iv)."* In state `d` (`f.code === 'd', f.r4 === 'full'`), line 11 (`R4`) *already* has `used < CAP`, `H-d` at line 166 is pushed off-screen by `unfold: ['find']` (`cur-L3-d.png`), and its replay stops at step 488 (before `lookup(8, -1)` enters `loop $probe`). Only after clicking `H-d`’s second chip (`relax (iv): drop && used < CAP`) to enter `dweak` (`cur-L3-dweak.png`) does `H-term` appear in `$find` with the negative measure staircase and the chip for the corrected clause `(iv)`.

## D. Soundness of the design itself

1. **Coherence of "a refinement is a region inside the shape" across predicate classes.**
   Drawing `{v : T | P(v)}` inside `T`’s shape is coherent when `P` is a **unary subset of a 1D ordered domain**: intervals (`[a, b)`), one-sided bounds (`0 <= v`), small sets (`{0, 1, 2}`), and small-modulus parity (`v % 2 == 0`). It breaks down for **relational, algebraic, and heap predicates**:
   - *Relational types ($\Sigma$-telescopes):* For `(result $h (i32 | $h + $h == $x))`, `v == gcd(a, b)` (`03-sample-programs.md` A3), or `(acc * pow(base, k)) % m == pow(b, e) % m` (A4), the static projection `{h : i32 | ∃x. h + h == x}` is `[-2³⁰, 2³⁰−1]`, erasing the relation, while the dynamic slice for a fixed `$x` is a singleton indistinguishable from the value dot. Revealingly, Lesson 1 leaves the output shape of `$half` on lines 10 and 21 **unrefined** (`30 × 11` px) and puts `= x/2` in a text chip instead.
   - *Non-interval bit-vectors and floats:* Bitmasks, modular arithmetic (`pow_mod`), and `f32`/`f64` error/NaN predicates cannot be drawn legibly as fence posts or teeth on an 84px bar.
   - *Memory invariants:* `R0`–`R4` are predicates on linear memory and ghost state, not subsets of a stack `i32`. Lesson 3 relies on text chips `R(o)`–`R(iv)` and a hand-crafted circular Memory Mirror widget; in a real editor, the checker cannot synthesize a domain-specific diagram from raw `forall s. load(...)` formulas without declarative view annotations.

2. **Where the design would break in a real CS 10N editor.**
   - **Local SMT counterexamples (CTIs) vs. whole-program replay traces.** A modular SMT solver (Z3/cvc5 in Wasm) falsifying a verification condition returns a **local state at the VC boundary** (such as `n = 3, r = 5` at a loop head), **not** an executable multi-call trace from `main` like `insert(3,30) · insert(11,110) · delete(3) · lookup(11,-1)`. Producing multi-call traces requires bounded model checking or fuzzing alongside SMT solving. Conversely, displaying a local CTI like `n = 3, r = 5` on the execution slider confuses students when `r = 5` is unreachable from `r = 0`.
   - **Horizontal scale.** Two refined `84 px` cups on a 2-operand instruction (`i32.gt_u` on line 17) already use `182 px` of the `190 px` fraction column; a 3-argument call with refined parameters (like `$pow_mod(b, e, m)` in A4) requires `274 px` and collides with `.facts`.

3. **What Lean, Dafny, and Liquid Haskell users would expect that is missing.**
   Without memory framing (`modifies`/`reads`), any helper performing `i32.store` (e.g., to an audio/canvas buffer) havocs linear memory and invalidates `R`. Unlike Dafny or Lean’s InfoView, `if`, `else`, and `br_if` lines show no active path-condition chips in the margin. Finally, omitting `gcd` (A3) and `pow_mod` (A4) leaves unspecified how recursive `@define` functions are unfolded at loop back-edges.

## E. Concrete, prioritized fixes

1. **Lesson 3’s Seed (a) prose and `H-a` note falsely claim that replaying the witness hits `💣` at line 142 before `⚖` at line 151, and a botched ternary (`? i : i`) in `View.replay()` prevents the replay button from jumping to the trap step.** Fix `View.replay()` to jump to `run.recs.length - 1` on traps, add the static spill on line 140 (`call $slot` with `i ∈ [1, 8]`), and update §5 and `H-a` to state that probe 1 increments `i` to `8` and fails the back-edge invariant `⚖` at step 143 (line 151) before trapping with `💣` at step 153 (line 142).
2. **Blamed edit lines marked via `fixAlso` (such as line 17 `i32.gt_s` in Lesson 2) have `S.st[key] === undefined`, causing their cup spill to render as unreplayed (`ghost`), their fraction rule to render as `proven`, and their refuted fact chip to stay hidden (`rv00-L2-crop.png`).** Update `View.statuses()`, `fracSVG()`, and `factsHTML()` so any line carrying a `fix` mark from a hole inherits that hole’s `refuted`/`ghost` status for its rule, cup spills, and fact chips.
3. **In Lesson 3’s Memory Mirror lens (`rv00-L3.png`), `boundary` only checks top-level call returns, so replaying Seed (b) to step 241 (`call $find (return)` inside read-only `$lookup`) grays out `R` as `"R open: mid-operation"` right next to prose claiming `"R stays true the whole time"`.** Treat every entry and return of a function declaring `(@requires R)` or `(@ensures R)` (and every step of read-only functions like `$find` and `$lookup`) as a checkpoint where `R`’s clauses are evaluated and colored.
4. **Clicking `▸ replay` on Seed (b) lands at step 241 (`lookup:72`), which is inside the folded `$lookup` body (`rv00-L3.png`), hiding the navy execution arrow `→` and current-line highlight.** In `View.replay()`, unfold the function containing the target step (`this.folded.delete(rec.fn)`) before painting.
5. **In Lesson 3’s Seed (d), `H-d` at line 166 is pushed off-screen by `unfold: ['find']` (`cur-L3-d.png`), its replay stops at step 488 before `lookup(8, -1)` enters `loop $probe`, and §5’s prose conflates state `d` with `dweak` (`cur-L3-dweak.png`).** Either start the Seed (d) preset directly in `dweak` (where `R4` omits `used < CAP` and `H-term` sits in `$find` with the negative measure staircase and the corrected clause `(iv)` chip) or walk the reader through clicking `relax (iv)`.
6. **In Lesson 2, accepting the second suggestion chip (`SPEC: $n < 2147395600`) and proving the nonlinear lemma closes all holes but leaves line 15’s overflow nub (`cap = 46340`) and line 17’s spill and `'refuted'` chip on screen.** Set `cap` in `isqrt.derive()` to `f.req === 'tight' ? 46339 : 46340` and mark line 17’s `= s` chip as `'proven'` when `f.req === 'tight' && known`.
7. **On multi-operand lines (`rv00-L2-crop.png` lines 15–17), cup 0’s overflow spill nub collides with cup 1’s left border and line 15’s dataflow curve cuts through line 16’s output shape.** Increase horizontal spacing in `View.geom()` after any input cup with a possible overflow nub (`x += w + NUB + 8`) and route multi-row stack curves outside the right edge of intermediate shapes.
8. **The refinement syntax switches silently between `$x`/`$root` binders (Lessons 1–2), bare `v` (§1 and Lesson 3), and subscripted stack names `v₇`/`v₁₅` (holes), relegating the explanation to §7 *Limits*.** Standardize on one binder convention across all listings, or add two sentences in §1 defining `v`, `$param`/`$result`, and `vₙ` stack names before they appear.
9. **§1 ("Six states, one cup") claims all six panels show the same `[1, 8]`-into-`[0, 8)` spill while secretly changing the `proven` panel to `[1, 7]`, and the `unknown` gutter glyph `𝄽?` never appears on `unknown` code lines (`cur-L2-nolemma.png` line 15).** Update the §1 caption to note that panel 1 shows `[1, 7]` fitting inside `[0, 8)` while the other five show verdicts on `[1, 8]`, and render `𝄽?` in the gutter of `unknown` instruction lines in `View.fill()`.
10. **In Lesson 2 Tab 1 (`noinv`), a Wasm reader is not told why `r = 5` can appear in a counterexample when line 3 sets `$r` to `0`, nor what `sq_lt_sq` states in Tab 3.** Add two sentences in §4 explaining that the verifier cuts loops at `loop $grow` and tests arbitrary `loop-head witnesses` satisfying only `@invariant`, and print `0 ≤ r < 46341 ⇐ r² < 46341²` on the `sq_lt_sq` chip label.
11. **Left-gutter causal trails (`rv00-s2.png`, `rv00-L2-crop.png`, `rv00-L3.png`) are never introduced in §§0–6 and visually mimic Codillon’s left-side control-flow frame lines.** Add a callout for the causal trail in §2’s diagram and legend, and draw the trail lanes only on hole hover/focus so the left gutter stays clear.
